# Concept art prompts — Ember & Blossom

Prompts for an image AI (Midjourney, ChatGPT / DALL·E, Leonardo, Firefly…), to generate target
art for the art pass and future zones. Each prompt is the **style block** + a **scene**.

**How to use**
1. Paste the style block, then one scene prompt. Use **16:9** unless the scene says otherwise.
2. If your tool takes an image reference (Midjourney `--sref`, ChatGPT "use this image's style"),
   attach `docs/art/reference.jpg`. It's the single best way to keep every image on-palette.
3. Generate 4 or more images per scene. Keep the 1 or 2 that read best as a *game* target (clear shapes,
   simple surfaces), not just the prettiest.
4. Save winners as `docs/art/concept_<scene-number>_<short-name>.png`, e.g. `concept_03_ashby.png`.
   Tell Claude which ones are the targets, and they become the reference for that zone's art pass.

---

## Style block (paste first, every time)

> Stylized 3D video game concept art, painterly and hand-sculpted, in the spirit of Ori and the
> Will of the Wisps, Zelda: Breath of the Wild and Journey. Chunky angular faceted shapes, very
> little surface noise, strong color blocking, clear silhouettes. Palette: obsidian, slate and
> twilight-indigo shadows; magma orange, sunset gold and blush pink highlights. Low golden-hour
> backlight with warm rim light on rock edges, soft bloom on glowing elements, atmospheric height
> fog, cinematic wide composition, high detail in light, simple in shadow.

**Negative / avoid** (Midjourney: `--no ...`; elsewhere add "Avoid: ..."):
> photorealism, gritty textures, noisy detail, text, watermark, UI, lens dirt, oversaturated
> neon, anime characters, cluttered composition

## 1. Home Cliffs — dawn variant of the hero shot
*Target for: the morning look of the starting area. Pair with the dusk reference.*
> A tiny wooden shack with a crooked stone chimney perched on the edge of a dark faceted cliff
> plateau, high above an endless sea of billowing cumulus clouds at dawn. A glowing gnarled cherry
> blossom tree leans out over the drop, its canopy made of many small soft pink blossom clusters
> lit from within, petals drifting in the breeze. Thin white waterfalls pour off tall angular rock
> walls on both sides and vanish into the clouds. Far on the horizon a lone volcano breathes a thin
> plume of smoke. Cool blue-lavender shadows, warm peach sunrise on cloud tops, morning mist.

## 2. Home Cliffs — night, sanctuary glow
*Target for: night lighting, the safe-zone feel of the sanctuary.*
> Night on a dark obsidian cliff ledge above a moonlit cloud sea. A sacred cherry blossom tree glows
> softly pink, casting a circle of warm light on the ground around a small shack with a lit window
> and a campfire. Fireflies and drifting petals. Beyond the light, deep indigo shadows, a sky full of
> stars, a faint orange glow from distant lava falls lighting the underside of the clouds. The volcano
> on the horizon glows red at its peak. Calm, safe, sacred atmosphere.

## 3. Ashby Village — the first settlement
*Target for: the village zone (Medieval Village kit + our palette).*
> A small medieval village nestled in a green valley under tall angular cliffs, about ten minutes'
> walk from the edge of the world. Timber-framed cottages with steep dark roofs, a stone mill wheel
> turning in a stream fed by waterfalls, a market square with cloth stalls and lanterns, a
> blacksmith's forge glowing orange. Golden late-afternoon light, long shadows, smoke curling from
> chimneys, cherry trees in bloom between houses. A dirt road winds up toward the cliffs in the
> background. Warm, lived-in, welcoming.

## 4. Falls Valley — vertical traversal
*Target for: the second zone; climbing, gliding, waterfall updrafts.*
> A deep gorge of towering faceted slate cliffs with dozens of waterfalls of every size pouring into
> misty turquoise pools far below. Rope bridges and wooden ledges cling to the rock. A small hero with
> a cloth glider rides a rising column of mist beside a huge waterfall. Rainbows in the spray, mossy
> green tops on dark rock, a glowing cherry blossom sanctuary tree on a high ledge. Sunlight in shafts
> through drifting mist, sense of huge vertical scale.

## 5. The Volcano — late-game biome approach
*Target for: the late-game zone, heat, danger, the far end of the palette.*
> A hero in a hooded cloak stands on a black obsidian ridge looking toward an enormous volcano. Rivers
> of glowing magma carve channels through jagged black rock; lava falls pour off cliffs into a sea of
> ash clouds lit orange from beneath. Ash and embers fill the air, the sky is smoky crimson fading to
> dark indigo. In the foreground, a single withered but still-glowing cherry blossom tree pushes back
> the heat with a soft pink aura, the last safe spot. Ominous, epic, but still beautiful.

## 6. Home growth — shack to keep (progression sheet)
*Target for: house-tier silhouettes; aspect **3:1** or a 4-panel sheet.*
> Concept sheet showing four stages of the same cliff-edge home, left to right: a tiny wooden shack;
> a cozy timber cottage with a vegetable garden; a long hall with a palisade, workshop and watchtower;
> a small stone keep with walls, a forge, banners and a courtyard. The same glowing cherry blossom tree
> stands beside each stage. Consistent camera angle, dusk lighting, clean readable silhouettes,
> labelled-free, plain background of clouds.

## 7. The Forge — crafting interior
*Target for: interiors and the weapon-crafting loop.*
> Interior of a rustic stone-and-timber forge at night. A blacksmith hero hammers a glowing orange
> sword blade on an anvil, sparks flying. A roaring furnace, bellows, racks of swords from wood to
> bronze to steel on the wall, a workbench with tools and ore. Warm orange firelight against deep
> indigo shadows coming through an open door onto a starry cliff. Chunky stylized props, cozy and
> purposeful.

## 8. Bandit raid at dusk — combat
*Target for: combat readability, enemy silhouettes, defending the base.*
> Action scene at dusk: a hero with a steel sword and round shield defends a wooden palisade gate on a
> cliff top against a group of bandits in ragged cloaks with torches and axes. Torchlight, flying
> embers, long shadows across the dirt, the glowing cherry blossom tree in the background. Dynamic
> over-the-shoulder third-person game camera angle, clear readable poses, stylized not gory.

## 9. Hero & weapon sheet
*Target for: the player character and the sword tiers; aspect **3:2**.*
> Character concept sheet for a young adventurer: hooded traveller's cloak, leather and cloth armour,
> warm scarf, simple and readable. Front, side and back views, plus a row of six swords showing the
> progression: rough wooden sword, stone club, bronze blade, iron sword, steel longsword, and an
> ember-forged sword with glowing magma cracks. Stylized chunky proportions, slightly oversized hands
> and boots. Neutral warm-grey background.

## 10. Cloud-sea glide — the money shot
*Target for: the moment players remember; traversal + vista.*
> A tiny hero glides on a cloth wing over an endless sea of pink and gold cumulus clouds at sunset,
> between towering black rock spires crowned with jagged obsidian blades. Thin lava falls pour from the
> spires and light the clouds from beneath in magma orange. A volcano on the horizon, the first
> stars appearing in an indigo sky, petals and embers swirling in the wind. Vast, free, breathtaking.

## Tips per tool
- **Midjourney:** append `--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250`. Add `--sref <url of reference.jpg>` to lock
  the style. Use `--no photorealism, text, watermark` for the negatives.
- **ChatGPT / DALL·E:** paste the style block + scene as one message and say "wide 16:9 image". Then
  iterate in plain language ("make the rocks darker and chunkier", "fewer, larger blossom clusters").
- **Any tool:** if results drift toward realism, add "low-poly stylized 3D render, simple shapes" to
  the front of the prompt.
